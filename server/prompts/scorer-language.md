## Brand Language

Minimalist brand language rules.

**Good:**

- Active ingredient + concentration led (e.g. "2% Salicylic Acid", "Pure 10% Niacinamide")
- Mechanism-focused copy — describes what the ingredient does at a molecular or cellular level
- Minimal — no filler words, no padding

**Bad:**

- Superlatives without substantiation ("best", "most powerful", "revolutionary", "game-changing")
- "Natural/clean/toxin-free" positioning — the brand explicitly rejects this framing
- Filler words and vague descriptors ("amazing", "incredible", "truly", "really")
- "Pure" used as a clean-beauty marketing claim (e.g. "pure and natural"). Note: "pure" describing ingredient grade is correct Minimalist language — do not flag it.
